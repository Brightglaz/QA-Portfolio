## Summary 
Combat ARs leave blood pools and trails when damaged, whereas it should've been oil-like, similar to previous patches.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. Start a mission where Combat ARs are often deployed, fighting factions such as Realware or ANCOM.
2. Damage a combat AR with piercing type weaponry.
3. Observer the trail and the pools left on the floor.

## Expected Results
Combat ARs leave oil, consistent with previous patches.

## Actual Result
Combat ARs leave blood.

## Evidence
![Combat AR leaving blood](QA%20Evidence/Blood_robot.png)

## Impact
Unlike blood, oil can catch on fire. So an oil trail is both a hazard and a possible tactical advantage for the player. If Combat ARs are meant to leave oil, this removes that hazard and tactic when fighting against them.

## Notes
Reported together with BUG-002 in the same Discord post. The developer expresses his thanks and announced that the problem is fixed in the next patch.
